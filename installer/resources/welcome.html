Welcome to RoomMesh @VERSION@

RoomMesh turns the Macs in a meeting room into one room audio system. Every Mac joins the meeting. RoomMesh picks the best microphone in the room, removes the echo, and plays the far end from a single room speaker.

What will be installed

  • RoomMesh App: the menu-bar app, in your Applications folder.
  • RoomMesh Audio Driver (optional): adds the RoomMesh Microphone and RoomMesh Speaker audio devices, in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/HAL.

Installing takes less than a minute. You'll need an administrator password.
