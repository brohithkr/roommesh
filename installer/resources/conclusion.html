RoomMesh is installed

RoomMesh has been opened and is in the menu bar. If it isn't, open it from your Applications folder.

Next steps

  1. Quit and reopen your browser and meeting apps (Meet in the browser, Zoom, Teams) if they were open during the install. Apps that were already running can't use the new RoomMesh devices until they restart.
  2. Allow Microphone and Local Network access when RoomMesh asks.
  3. On one Mac, choose Connect next to another Mac under Nearby Macs. On the other Mac, check that the 6-digit code matches, then choose Join.
  4. In Meet, Zoom or Teams, select RoomMesh Microphone and RoomMesh Speaker on every Mac.

Uninstalling

Open the RoomMesh Installer disk image and double-click Uninstall RoomMesh.command. It removes the app, the audio driver and the installer receipts, then restarts macOS audio.
