Before you install

Requirements

  • macOS @MIN_OS@ or later.
  • @ARCH_REQUIREMENT@
  • All Macs in the room on the same local network.

The audio driver

The RoomMesh Audio Driver adds two audio devices: RoomMesh Microphone and RoomMesh Speaker. Meeting apps use them like any other microphone and speaker. RoomMesh decides what they carry, based on this Mac's role in the room, so you never have to change the meeting app's settings when roles change.

A Mac needs the driver to act as the room coordinator. Installing or removing it restarts macOS audio for about a second. If you skip it now, RoomMesh can install it later.

After installing

  1. When RoomMesh asks, allow Microphone and Local Network access.
  2. On every Mac in the room, in Meet, Zoom or Teams, select RoomMesh Microphone as the microphone and RoomMesh Speaker as the speaker.
